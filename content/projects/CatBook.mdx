A social media website for Tigers, Lions, Cats, and other felines.  

Contrary to popular belief, cats need friends too. That's why we created a social media application for anyone feline a bit lonely.  
We were certain that many cats would be purrsuaded by this idea.

We used **ExpressJS/MongoDB** for the backend/database and **React/Bootstrap** for the frontend.  
The project was very successful in implementing functionality commonly seen within social media applications.
