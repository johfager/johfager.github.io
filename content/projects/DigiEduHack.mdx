AdaptaTec was the winning project in the **EU-sponsored DigiEduHack hackathon**, where we competed against over **200+ teams globally**.  
The hackathon took place during my exchange in Mexico, and our team was honored to be named **global winners**.

### **The Challenge**
The goal was to create innovative solutions for education. Our team focused on helping **neurodivergent students**, such as those with ADHD, dyslexia, or autism, who often face challenges with traditional learning methods.

### **The Solution**
We developed a concept for **AdaptaTec**, a study platform designed to:
- Adapt content to individual learning needs.
- Provide visual aids and interactive tools to improve comprehension.
- Help students stay focused and engaged.
